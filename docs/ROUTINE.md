**Which run (ET date):**
- **1st of the month: monthly edition.** The whole previous month (through its last day), every closer's card posted to **#sales-reports** (`C0C5CGGGE1M`) for Jay's 1:1s. The card adds a **<New month> plan** table: the new month's weeks with each week's share of the month and its cash, closes and slots targets, so Jay can set the month's goal with the closer. Its goal and weekly split come from `data/targets/<new month>.json`. No DMs from this edition.

- **Wednesday: Wednesday edition**, month to date through Tuesday, one DM per closer.
- When the 1st is a Monday or Wednesday, run only the monthly edition: the #sales-reports post, no DMs to closers that day.

**Read-only rule:** never write to or change anything in Google Sheets, Calendly, the tracker, Slack history or any other source. ## Steps

1. **Edition and dates (ET).** Every number on a Monday or 1st-of-month card lines up with the Weekly Scorecard's finished weeks (Sheila, Nov 9): the build ends the card at the last finished week, so pull data through the dates below and let the build cut it. From December 2026 weeks end on Sunday, so a Monday card runs through Sunday as pulled. In November 2026 (Sun-Sat weeks) a Monday card's month to date (title date, cash, chart, closes, live calls, average revenue, slots, goal to date, pace) ends on Saturday, while Sunday belongs to the next week: the highlighted current week's "so far" cash and closes include Sunday, and the month-to-date numbers pick Sunday up from Wednesday's card. The management view uses the same window, so it shows exactly what closers were sent. The build's flags say when it cut the window ("Cards cover month to date through ..."); the card title then shows that date. Monday: month to date through Sunday; the snapshot's `editions.monday.week_days` = last Mon..Sun. Wednesday: month to date through Tuesday; `editions.wednesday.week_days` = this Mon..Sun. Monthly (the 1st): through the last day of the previous month; `editions.monthly.week_days` = the last 7 days of that month; it reads the Monday-style blocks (`mtd`, `wk`, `business_days_elapsed_mon`, `days_mon`). `through_key` = the last day of the window ("MM-DD"), `slots_post_day` = today. When the window is in the previous month, use that month throughout (its sheets rows, tracker month and Messi posts).
2. **Snapshot.** Start from `data/sample-2026-10-19.json` for the shape (keep `closers`), write `data/scorecard-<today>.json`, and replace every data block for this run's edition: `month` (label, prev_label, business_days, business_days_elapsed_<mon|wed>, days_<mon|wed>), `editions.<edition>`, `activity`, `slots` (keyed by edition), `messi_slots`, `slots_booked`, `daily_eod`, `daily_st`, `eod_inferred`, `daily_closes`. Drop blocks the card no longer reads (`pipeline_leads`, `pipeline_last_week`, `noshow_confirm`).
3. **Booked / showed.** EOD Tracker Sheet1 rows for the month to date as `daily_eod[key]["MM-DD"] = [scheduled, showed, no-show, cancelled, rescheduled]`; Sales Tracker `Inputs` (A Closer, E Date, F Sched, G Live) as `daily_st[key]["MM-DD"] = [sched, live]`. A blank EOD "showed" = scheduled − no-show − cancel − reschedule, listed in `eod_inferred[key]`. Name maps: EOD "Jordan Zadrozny" / Sales Tracker "Jay Z" → JZ; "Adam L" → Adam.
4. **Revenue, closes, cash.** Sales Tracker `Inputs` (J Close 1, V Rev. Gen., W Cash Collected) into `activity[key]` arrays `[booked calls, live calls, offers, closes, revenue generated, cash collected]` for `mtd` (Monday, monthly) or `mtd2` (Wednesday), and `wk` (Monday, monthly) or `tw` (Wednesday) for the edition week. Per-day Close 1 as `daily_closes[key]["MM-DD"] = n` (days with closes only) through `through_key`; it must add up to the month-to-date closes. Per-day revenue generated (column V) the same way as `daily_rev[key]["MM-DD"] = dollars` (days with revenue only); it must add up to the month-to-date revenue. A Monday card in a month whose weeks end on Saturday stops at Saturday (below), and needs these per-day numbers to cut closes and average revenue there.
5. **Cash.** `node src/fetch-tracker.mjs data/scorecard-<today>.json <YYYY-MM-DD of through_key>` (actual cash only). If `all_checks_pass` is false it becomes a flag.
5a. **Closer targets.** Every goal on the card (month goal, closes target, goal to date, pace badge, weekly targets, next month's plan) comes from Sheila's closer-facing file `data/targets/<YYYY-MM>.json`, never from the 2M tracker (its numbers are management-facing). The month being reported needs its file; the monthly edition also needs the new month's. Copy what you used into the snapshot as `closer_targets["YYYY-MM"]`. If a needed file is missing, or the build flags it (wrong number of weekly shares, shares not adding up to 100%), DM Sheila what is missing and send no cards that run.
6. **Slots.** Messi corrections: if Messi posts a correction for a day, use the corrected numbers for that day. A post where every closer shows 0 open slots with no correction is bad data: leave that day out and flag it. For October 2026 set `messi_slots_from: "10-21"` (Sheila, Oct 30: posts before Messi's switch to the team discovery links, first seen in the Oct 21 post, are less accurate). From November the count is consistent; leave `messi_slots_from` out.
   Every Messi 6am post in #sales-reports this month → `messi_slots["MM-DD"][key]` (set `messi_post_hour_et: 6`) (open start times, next 7 days; today's post included) and `slots.<edition>[key]` = today's numbers. Booked: pull every Calendly scheduled event, active and canceled, for each closer from the first post of the month through 7 days after today, save as `{pulled_at, closers: {key: [{name, start, status, created, canceled_at}]}}` (`canceled_at` = `cancellation.created_at`), then `node src/booked-slots.mjs data/scorecard-<today>.json <events.json>`.
7. **Render.** `node src/build.mjs data/scorecard-<today>.json out <edition>`, then the management view: `node src/management.mjs data/scorecard-<today>.json out <edition>` (`out/<edition>-management.png`: every closer's numbers from the same cards on one page). Its slots columns mirror the cards' Weekly Scorecard rows (Sheila, Nov 9), so management sees exactly what each closer was sent: the last finished week's daily average (header names the week and dates) and the next 7 days from today's Messi post, booked · open. On the 1st there is no current week, so the second column is the month average (the cards' month row). Look at two or three PNGs before sending: title date, weekly rows add up (closes in the rows = closes in the month row; on a November 2026 Monday card the current week also counts Sunday, which the month row leaves out), no blank tiles, and for two closers the management slots numbers equal their card's last finished week and current-week slots.
