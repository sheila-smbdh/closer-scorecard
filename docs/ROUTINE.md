# Closer Scorecard routine

Runs at 7:14am ET on the 1st of every month and every Monday and Wednesday (`CRON_TZ=America/New_York 14 7 1 * 1,3`: day-of-month and day-of-week together fire on either), after Messi's 6am slot post. **Paused until Sheila turns it on (sending starts in November).** Decisions behind each step are in `docs/HANDOFF.md`; metric definitions are in `README.md`.

**Which run (ET date):**
- **1st of the month: monthly edition.** The whole previous month (through its last day), every closer's card posted to **#sales-reports** (`C0C5CGGGE1M`) for Jay's 1:1s. The card adds a **<New month> plan** table: the new month's weeks with each week's share of the month and its cash, closes and slots targets, so Jay can set the month's goal with the closer. Its goal and weekly split come from `data/targets/<new month>.json`. No DMs from this edition.
- **First DMs: Monday Nov 9, 2026** (Sheila, Oct 30). Monday and Wednesday runs before Nov 9 (Nov 2, 4) do nothing. The Nov 1 thread post still goes out.
- **Monday: Monday edition**, month to date through Sunday, one DM per closer.
- **Wednesday: Wednesday edition**, month to date through Tuesday, one DM per closer.
- When the 1st is a Monday or Wednesday, run only the monthly edition: the #sales-reports post, no DMs to closers that day.

**Read-only rule:** never write to or change anything in Google Sheets, Calendly, the tracker, Slack history or any other source. The only outputs are the #sales-reports post on the 1st (one message, cards in its thread), one DM per closer on Monday and Wednesday, the flags DM to Sheila (Slack `U0BM6J8KM32`), and the run's snapshot committed to this repo.

**Who gets a card:** all 15 closers in `closers` (Owen, Adam, JZ, VeJohn, Julien, Nick, Omar, Harry, Solomon, Daniel, Kevin, Oliver, Tyler, Yobani, Quinn), each by Slack DM to the `slack` id in the roster. Oliver, Tyler, Yobani and Quinn start in November (Sheila, Oct 30): their goals come from the targets file like everyone's. Yobani and Quinn were setters; find their Sales Tracker and EOD rows by first name, and a closer with no rows yet reads as zeros. A closer with no goal in the month's targets file is not sent a card that month; list them in the flags.

## Steps

1. **Edition and dates (ET).** Monday: month to date through Sunday; the snapshot's `editions.monday.week_days` = last Mon..Sun. Wednesday: month to date through Tuesday; `editions.wednesday.week_days` = this Mon..Sun. Monthly (the 1st): through the last day of the previous month; `editions.monthly.week_days` = the last 7 days of that month; it reads the Monday-style blocks (`mtd`, `wk`, `business_days_elapsed_mon`, `days_mon`). `through_key` = the last day of the window ("MM-DD"), `slots_post_day` = today. When the window is in the previous month, use that month throughout (its sheets rows, tracker month and Messi posts).
2. **Snapshot.** Start from `data/sample-2026-10-19.json` for the shape (keep `closers`), write `data/scorecard-<today>.json`, and replace every data block for this run's edition: `month` (label, prev_label, business_days, business_days_elapsed_<mon|wed>, days_<mon|wed>), `editions.<edition>`, `activity`, `slots` (keyed by edition), `messi_slots`, `slots_booked`, `daily_eod`, `daily_st`, `eod_inferred`, `daily_closes`. Drop blocks the card no longer reads (`pipeline_leads`, `pipeline_last_week`, `noshow_confirm`).
3. **Booked / showed.** EOD Tracker Sheet1 rows for the month to date as `daily_eod[key]["MM-DD"] = [scheduled, showed, no-show, cancelled, rescheduled]`; Sales Tracker `Inputs` (A Closer, E Date, F Sched, G Live) as `daily_st[key]["MM-DD"] = [sched, live]`. A blank EOD "showed" = scheduled − no-show − cancel − reschedule, listed in `eod_inferred[key]`. Name maps: EOD "Jordan Zadrozny" / Sales Tracker "Jay Z" → JZ; "Adam L" → Adam.
4. **Revenue, closes, cash.** Sales Tracker `Inputs` (J Close 1, V Rev. Gen., W Cash Collected) into `activity[key]` arrays `[booked calls, live calls, offers, closes, revenue generated, cash collected]` for `mtd` (Monday, monthly) or `mtd2` (Wednesday), and `wk` (Monday, monthly) or `tw` (Wednesday) for the edition week. Per-day Close 1 as `daily_closes[key]["MM-DD"] = n` (days with closes only) through `through_key`; it must add up to the month-to-date closes.
5. **Cash.** `node src/fetch-tracker.mjs data/scorecard-<today>.json <YYYY-MM-DD of through_key>` (actual cash only). If `all_checks_pass` is false it becomes a flag.
5a. **Closer targets.** Every goal on the card (month goal, closes target, goal to date, pace badge, weekly targets, next month's plan) comes from Sheila's closer-facing file `data/targets/<YYYY-MM>.json`, never from the 2M tracker (its numbers are management-facing). The month being reported needs its file; the monthly edition also needs the new month's. Copy what you used into the snapshot as `closer_targets["YYYY-MM"]`. If a needed file is missing, or the build flags it (wrong number of weekly shares, shares not adding up to 100%), DM Sheila what is missing and send no cards that run.
6. **Slots.** Every Messi 6am post in #sales-reports this month → `messi_slots["MM-DD"][key]` (set `messi_post_hour_et: 6`) (open start times, next 7 days; today's post included) and `slots.<edition>[key]` = today's numbers. Booked: pull every Calendly scheduled event, active and canceled, for each closer from the first post of the month through 7 days after today, save as `{pulled_at, closers: {key: [{name, start, status, created, canceled_at}]}}` (`canceled_at` = `cancellation.created_at`), then `node src/booked-slots.mjs data/scorecard-<today>.json <events.json>`.
7. **Render.** `node src/build.mjs data/scorecard-<today>.json out <edition>`. Look at two or three PNGs before sending: title date, weekly rows add up (closes in the rows = closes in the month row), no blank tiles.
8. **Send.**
   - Monday / Wednesday: for each closer, upload `out/<edition>-<key>.png` to their DM with one plain line: "Your scorecard, month to date through <Weekday, Month Dth>." No emojis, no other text.
   - **Nov 1, 2026 only:** do not post a new message. Upload every card into Sheila's existing thread "Open this thread to get all the closer scorecards" (channel `D0BNJ9DNKNE`, `thread_ts` `1793394580.080129`), in roster order, with the closer's full name as the comment. Nothing else in that conversation.
   - Monthly (from Dec 1): post one message to #sales-reports (`C0C5CGGGE1M`): "<Month> closer scorecards are in <@U06Q63DTS13>" (<Month> = the month the cards cover, the one just ended; U06Q63DTS13 is Jay DeCristofaro). Then upload each closer's `out/monthly-<key>.png` **in that message's thread** (`thread_ts` = its ts), in roster order, with the closer's full name as the comment. Nothing goes to the channel itself except that one message. On the 1st a closer with no goal for the month just ended (Oliver and Tyler on Nov 1) is still included when the new month's file has their goal: their card shows the month's actuals and the new month's plan.
9. **Flags.** DM Sheila `out/<edition>-flags.md` every run.
10. **Save.** Commit `data/scorecard-<today>.json` and push to `main`. Do not commit `out/` (the images go out by Slack).
11. **Fail loudly.** If any source fails (sheet, Slack history, Calendly, tracker), DM Sheila what failed and send no cards that run (no channel post, no DMs). Never send partial cards.
