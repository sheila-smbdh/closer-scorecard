From the EOD Tracker, the Sales Tracker 2026 (Inputs tab), the Pipeline Review sheet, the 2M tracker, Close and Calendly, times in ET. Pace: cash collected vs what the 2M tracker expects by this day on its month curve, which is back-loaded (more cash lands late in the month); month end at this pace = % of pace × month target. Team median: the middle value among fully ramped closers. Open slots: discovery start times Calendly offers over the next 7 days; the week figure averages each day's count. Live calls: calls that showed, from your EOD reports (Sales Tracker for any day without one). Average revenue: revenue generated ÷ closes, month to date. Projected closes: closes so far + 80% of open High Confidence + 50% of open Soft Verbal / Reengaged leads in your current-month pipeline; Working is shown, not counted. Closes needed: month target ÷ $17,500.
