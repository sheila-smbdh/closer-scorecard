Closes needed: month target ÷ $17,500 average per close. Path to target: gap = month target − cash collected; your pipeline is open leads at their column D amounts (a lead with no amount counts as $17.5K); new closes at $17.5K each; calls needed use your month-to-date show rate (live ÷ booked) and close rate (closes ÷ live calls), or the team median until you have a close.
